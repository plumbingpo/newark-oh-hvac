Restaurant Services

Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

Few buildings work an HVAC system harder than a restaurant. Every burner and fryer adds heat, the exhaust hoods pull conditioned air outside nonstop, and the makeup air system has to replace that air without throwing off the building's pressure. Newark HVAC services, sizes, and repairs the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep the kitchen and dining room comfortable, and we plan visits around your operating hours.

Comfort and air balance are critical issues for any food-service operator. A kitchen running negative pressure pulls dining-room air the wrong way, drags outdoor air through every door, and makes staff miserable during peak service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and airflow your operation depends on, with written service records that show a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors. We provide detailed reports and service logs after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

Replacement and maintenance plans are planned with owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits your operating schedule. When a job can't happen during service, we do it after close, keeping your kitchen cooking and the dining room comfortable in the meantime.

Safe Installation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. Those are real risks for a commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Every installation and inspection we perform covers heat exchanger integrity, venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge, condensate drainage, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

The paperwork matters as much as the repair. We keep a service binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, giving you proof of due diligence for landlords, insurers, and auditors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, gets a more frequent visit schedule.

Emergency Service

Commercial Emergency HVAC Response in Newark

Every hour without climate control costs a business money. For a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. On a retail floor, it empties the aisles on a summer afternoon. In a server room, it risks overheated equipment, and in a leased building it leads to tenant disputes. Newark HVAC offers fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Newark and surrounding areas, dispatching an experienced technician quickly when you call.

Our technicians are equipped to handle any commercial HVAC emergency, including failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, and control board lockouts. We prioritize diagnosing the fault and restoring heating or cooling as quickly as possible, then put maintenance and monitoring in place to keep it from happening again. Every commercial response includes thorough documentation for your service records and facility files.
